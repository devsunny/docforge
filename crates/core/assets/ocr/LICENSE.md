# OCR model attribution

The files in this directory are the pre-trained OCR models from
[`ocrs-models`](https://github.com/robertknight/ocrs-models):

- `text-detection.onnx`
- `text-recognition.onnx`

They are trained on the
[HierText dataset](https://github.com/google-research-datasets/hiertext) by
Google Research and are licensed under **CC BY-SA 4.0**.
